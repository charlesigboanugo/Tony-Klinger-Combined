Each Commons image is the original resized to 2200px on its long edge and
stored as WebP (never enlarged), as with the rest of the library. They were AVIF until
2026-09-30, when every source became WebP because the image optimizer never resizes AVIF;
`_origin-hashes.json` keeps each file's original AVIF hash so its storage path is unchanged.

## Podcast and interview artwork (added 2026-09-25)

artwork for the appearance, used — as album art is in a discography — only to
identify the show Tony appeared on, beside a link to or a recording of that
appearance. Rights stay with each show; the card credits it. Each source was
checked to be the show in question (for Follow Your Dream, by matching the
podcast site's episode list against the directory feed). Wide logos were padded
to a square in their own background colour; nothing was enlarged.
